Every block carries a Proof-of-Quantum-Work claim: a 6-qubit parameterised circuit is prepared from the previous block hash, the transaction Merkle root and the mining nonce, and it must concentrate probability on the target outcome well above the 1.5625% random baseline. Click a block to see the raw claim, the Merkle root and the state fingerprint, all of which any node can recompute. Two assets settle here: QSAM is transferable and capped at 1,000,000,000; QBTC is capped at 21,000,000 and can never move, which is what makes "not for sale" a property of the ledger rather than a promise.
